# Family Care Blueprint

Family Care Blueprint is an internal content analytics platform built around the Family Care Blueprint Pinterest Business account.

The platform uses the Pinterest API v5 to pull pin performance data — impressions, saves, clicks, and outbound traffic — and surfaces that data as structured reports for content strategy and editorial planning.

---

## About

This tool is operated privately by the Family Care Blueprint team for internal business intelligence purposes. It is not a public-facing product or service.
